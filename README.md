OneUI Gallery Port Magisk Module (Edited by codenamezeroseven)
Supported on Android 16 QPR 1 & 2

This Magisk module brings the OneUI Gallery app experience to your device running Android 16 QPR 1 and 2. edited by codenamezeroseven, this module ports the highly praised Samsung Gallery app to non-Samsung devices, giving you access to its rich features and smooth interface.

With this port, you can enjoy seamless photo and video organization, powerful editing tools, and enhanced media browsing features, all while maintaining the stock look and feel of OneUI on your device. Whether you’re looking for easy album management, advanced editing options, or quick access to your cloud photos, this module ensures you get the full OneUI experience without needing a Samsung phone.

Key Features:

OneUI Gallery on non-Samsung devices: Get Samsung’s signature Gallery app on your Android 16 device.

Full compatibility with Android 16 QPR 1 & 2: Optimized for smooth performance on these specific Android versions.

Rich photo and video management: Group, tag, and organize media with ease.

Advanced editing tools: Enhance your photos with built-in editing features.

Cloud integration: Sync and backup your photos for added convenience.

Stock OneUI experience: Keep the smooth, minimal OneUI aesthetic without sacrificing functionality.

Installation Instructions:

Flash the module using Magisk Manager.

Reboot your device.

Enjoy the OneUI Gallery app on your phone!

Note: This module is only supported on devices running Android 16 QPR 1 & 2. Flashing on other versions may cause issues.
